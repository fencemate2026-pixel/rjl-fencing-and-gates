# RJL social publishing plan

Official website-linked profiles are recorded in search-publishing.json. Public website links are active; authenticated social scheduling requires Metricool to be installed and connected to BOTH RJL brands. Do not claim scheduled posts without provider confirmation of platform, brand and time. Never create duplicate profiles or use personal accounts. Add LinkedIn only after the actual company page and publishing access are verified.

Initial test windows: commercial Tuesday08:30; residential Wednesday19:00, Australia/Melbourne including daylight saving. These are audience-based assumptions, not measured best times. Commercial: facilities, strata/body corporate, procurement, builders and commercial property decision-makers. Residential: homeowners planning fencing, gates, pool barriers or retaining work in RJL's real service area. No paid advertising or paid audience targeting is activated.

After each article is live, prepare distinct Facebook and Instagram copy with an Australian takeaway and a relevant enquiry link. Use approved existing project photos with truthful captions. Facebook can link directly; Instagram caption URLs are not clickable, so use an existing authorised bio/story link without blindly replacing profile destinations. Do not paste identical long blog text everywhere.

Track public campaign labels only: utm_source=facebook or instagram, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=blog_YYYYMMDD_slug, optional utm_content=platform_format. No customer details. Compare engagement, link clicks, qualified enquiries and actual completed-job contribution. After four or more posts per brand/platform, use account audience/activity data and enquiry outcomes to adjust timing. Keep a publication log and avoid duplicates.
