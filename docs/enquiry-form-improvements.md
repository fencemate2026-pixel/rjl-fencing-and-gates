# Enquiry form improvements

Commercial enquiries now appear immediately after the contact hero, with a direct jump link. Forms accept an optional site address/postcode and expandable site type, priority, timing, site/asset counts and photos/plans/asset registers. All fields are declared in static HTML for Netlify; uploads use multipart POST and the existing7MB combined limit. Consent and urgent telephone guidance appear before submission. The free report remains a request, not an instant confirmed booking. A duplicated maintenance option and the visible honeypot are corrected.

Residential quote requests retain the short initial step and existing photo, plan and designer handover. Optional expandable measurements, timing and budget help qualification without inventing price bands or requiring a known budget.
