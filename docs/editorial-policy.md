# RJL article publishing policy

Jazz has authorised one original Australian-focused article per site each week through ChatGPT scheduled tasks. Commercial: Monday mornings, starting9November2026. Residential: Tuesday mornings, starting10November2026. Timezone: Australia/Melbourne.

Commercial audience: facilities, strata, procurement, builders and commercial property owners. Residential audience: homeowners within the site's actual service area. Keep topics and copy distinct. Use current Australian primary sources and Australian English, metric units and AUD. Identify the state when discussing rules. Verify manufacturer claims and Australian availability. Do not guess Australian Standards clauses or assert universal Australian permit rules.

Prefer a verified relevant development; otherwise answer a useful evergreen Australian customer question. Never label an old product or evergreen explainer as new news. Explain practical decisions and include a checklist and a relevant RJL service link. Avoid duplication, keyword stuffing, thin suburb pages and padding. No fabricated jobs, quotes, outcomes, savings, human review, qualifications or prices. Do not disclose customer details or private records. Use an honest organisational byline and disclose research assistance without claiming expert review. Published source links are required. Images must be correctly labelled references unless they actually depict the described RJL project.

Use existing article templates. Update the blog index, sitemap, canonical metadata and matching BlogPosting dates. Update RSS only if a feed exists. Log each topic, URL, sources and publication date in docs/content-log.md. Preserve existing dates on unchanged articles. Run repository checks, inspect the intended Netlify preview and merge only after it passes. Verify the live URL. Record blockers honestly and preserve drafts if publication cannot be completed. No ranking guarantees, purchased backlinks or ad spend changes.

The free commercial on-site maintenance report remains free with no maintenance-contract requirement. Residential 3D designer promotion and all design-to-quote handovers must remain.
