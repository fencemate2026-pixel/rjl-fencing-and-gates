Our story

A local trade business that kept building its capability.

RJL's published website history traces the family's fencing work to 1990. The official RJL Facebook and Instagram profiles describe the fencing business as established in 1997. The current company record for RJL FENCING PTY. LTD., ABN 82 159 036 781, is active from 1 July 2012. These are different milestones, so they are presented separately.

The residential division works across timber and Colorbond boundaries, custom steel gates, pool barriers, gate automation and retaining walls. The aim is straightforward: define the site properly, communicate the scope clearly and leave a result that belongs at the home.
