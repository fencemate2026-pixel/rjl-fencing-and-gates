Home/Steel fencing & gates
Custom steel capability

Made for the opening—not cut down from a standard size.

RJL designs and fabricates steel fencing and gates around the actual driveway, house, ground conditions and access pattern. Slat, blade, tubular, picket, solid-panel and laser-cut details can be coordinated across the full front boundary.

Powder-coated finishes provide colour consistency and long-term protection. Gate frames, hinges, tracks, posts, stops and automation are considered as one system rather than added separately at the end.

  • Sliding, swing, cantilever and pedestrian gates
  • Slat, blade, picket, tubular and privacy-panel fencing
  • Custom fabrication and powder-coated finishes
  • House numbers, letterboxes, intercom and keypad integration
  • Designed, fabricated and installed by one RJL team

Automatic gates

Reliable movement starts with the gate itself.

Automation is planned around gate weight, balance, travel, wind exposure, vehicle movement and how the household wants to enter. RJL can coordinate suitable CAME or Beninca equipment with remotes, keypads, intercoms, safety devices and smartphone access where required.

  • Sliding and swing-gate motors sized for the fabricated gate
  • Photocell safety beams and obstacle-detection options
  • Remote controls, PIN keypads, intercoms and app-based entry
  • Manual release and battery-backup options for power outages
  • Motorising suitable existing gates following a site assessment

Steel fencing FAQs

Planning a complete front boundary.

Start with the driveway opening, pedestrian access, preferred privacy and any automation or intercom requirements. RJL can then plan the fence, posts, gates and access controls as one installation.

Explore fencing in Bundoora →
Does RJL make custom steel fencing and gates in Bundoora?

Yes. RJL measures the site, designs the fence and gate as one entry system, fabricates the steelwork and installs it for homes in Bundoora and across Melbourne’s north and north-east.

Can a custom steel driveway gate be automated?

Yes. Sliding and swing gate automation can be designed with the gate so its frame, weight, hardware, motor, safety devices and access controls work together.

Which steel fence and gate styles are available?

Options include vertical blade, horizontal slat, steel picket, tubular, privacy-panel and laser-cut designs, with powder-coated colours selected to suit the property.

Can RJL quote the front fence, pedestrian gate and driveway gate together?

Yes. Quoting the complete front boundary together helps align heights, spacing, finishes, posts, letterboxes, access control and gate automation.
