Residential FAQ

Can I design a fence using a photo of my own house?

Yes, our 3D fence visualizer allows you to upload any smartphone photo of your yard and immediately overlay custom fence designs onto your actual property boundaries.

What types of residential fences can I preview?

You can customize and preview a wide range of styles, including timber paling, colorbond steel, PVC/vinyl privacy panels, ornamental aluminium, and matching pedestrian gates.

Is the 3D fence design tool free to use?

Our basic residential photo visualizer is completely free to use online, allowing you to test different heights, colors, and materials without any upfront commitment.
