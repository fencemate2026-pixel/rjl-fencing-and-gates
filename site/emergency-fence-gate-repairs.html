Emergency FAQ

Questions people ask in an emergency.

Is the emergency line really 24/7?

Yes. The RJL Group Rapid Response line, 0400 101 132, is answered 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, and two team members are on standby day and night.

What counts as a gate or fence emergency?

A gate stuck open or closed, an automatic gate that will not lock, a gate that has come off its track or hinges, or a fence knocked down by a vehicle, storm or break-in that leaves the property open or unsafe.

Can you make the site safe straight away?

Yes. RJL brings temporary fence panels and the gear to secure the opening on the first visit, then quotes the permanent repair.

Which number do I call for a normal quote?

For non-urgent work and quotes call 0412 467 840 or use the quote form. Keep 0400 101 132 for emergencies.
