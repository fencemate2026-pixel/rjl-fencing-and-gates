Home/Gate automation
Gate first, motor second

A motor cannot fix a gate that does not move properly.

Reliable automation begins with a straight, supported and freely moving gate. RJL considers the gate weight, balance, travel, wind exposure, duty cycle, driveway movement, power and household access before selecting equipment.

  • Automatic sliding gates and double or single swing gates
  • CAME or Beninca equipment selected to suit the gate and duty
  • Photocell beams and other site-appropriate safety devices
  • Remote controls, keypads, intercoms and smartphone access options
  • Manual release and available battery-backup options
  • Assessment of existing gates before retrofitting automation

Gate automation FAQ

Questions homeowners ask before motorising a gate.

Can RJL automate an existing driveway gate?

Sometimes. RJL first checks the gate condition, alignment, balance, posts, hinges or track, stops, travel, power and required safety devices. A weak or poorly moving gate should be corrected before a motor is fitted.

What access controls can be added to an automatic gate?

Depending on the system, options can include remotes, PIN keypads, intercoms, smartphone access, timed access and other controls suited to the household.

What happens if the power fails?

Automatic gate systems should include an accessible manual-release process. Battery-backup options may also be available for suitable motors and usage requirements.

What safety devices can an automatic driveway gate use?

The design may use photocell safety beams, obstacle detection, suitable stops, warning devices and control logic. The required arrangement depends on the gate and site risk assessment.
