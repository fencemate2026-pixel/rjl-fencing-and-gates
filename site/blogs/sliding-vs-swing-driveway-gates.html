Sliding or swing gate? Choosing the right driveway entry

A practical comparison of sliding and swing driveway gates for Melbourne homes, including space, slope, automation and safety.

RJL homeowner advicePublished 21 August 2026Discuss your site ↗All advice ↗

A driveway gate should be selected around the actual site geometry. The best-looking option on paper may not suit the slope, side room, vehicle waiting space or the way people use the entry every day.

A sliding gate often suits

  • Driveways where the gate must not swing into vehicle space.
  • Wider openings with sufficient clear run-back beside the driveway.
  • Sites where wind loading and controlled movement need close attention.

A swing gate often suits

  • Traditional homes and entries where the gate form is part of the architecture.
  • Sites without adequate side room for a sliding leaf.
  • Driveways with enough level, clear area for the leaves to open safely.

Plan the automation at design stage

Motor capacity, hinges or track, drainage, power, manual release, safety beams, access controls and gate stops should be coordinated before fabrication. That produces a cleaner and more reliable installation.
