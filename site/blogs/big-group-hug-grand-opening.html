Big Group Hug’s public acknowledgement of RJL Fencing

Independent public records of RJL Fencing’s support for Big Group Hug, alongside RJL’s account of its community involvement.

RJL community storyPublished 29 May 2026Discuss your site ↗All advice ↗

Big Group Hug is a Melbourne charity that provides essential items to babies and children experiencing hardship. Big Group Hug publicly acknowledged support from RJL Fencing, and its 2018–19 annual report also records RJL Fencing’s generosity.

The August event brought the charity, local supporters, suppliers and council representatives together around a practical goal: helping Victorian children and families access the essentials they need.

Learn about Big Group Hug and its work ↗

A public acknowledgement of practical help

Big Group Hug’s own public post recorded that Jarrod and the RJL team contacted the charity and offered help when support was requested.

RJL’s account describes broader event, supplier and logistics support. That detail is presented as RJL’s published account; the charity’s public post independently verifies that the team offered help.

Big Group Hug’s public acknowledgement of RJL ↗

Recorded in Big Group Hug’s annual report

Big Group Hug’s 2018–19 annual report also credits the generosity of RJL Fencing while describing the development of the Bendigo Bank Kids Corner. That independent record matters to us because it captures the practical difference local organisations can make when they combine their resources.

Big Group Hug 2018–19 Annual Report ↗

Backing grassroots football and cricket

RJL also sponsors local football and cricket and supports club events. Our community involvement has included cricket partnerships with Northern Socials Cricket Club and Thomastown United Cricket Club, alongside support for local football leagues and clubs.

For RJL, this is part of being a long-standing local family business: contributing to the clubs, volunteers and community organisations that give people a place to connect.
