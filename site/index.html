All common questions ↗

Should I choose timber or Colorbond fencing in Bundoora?

Timber offers flexible detailing and a natural finish. Colorbond offers full privacy, consistent colour and low maintenance. Access, levels, existing structures and the result you want should decide the system.

Read the answer →

Is a sliding gate or double swing gate better for my driveway?

Sliding gates need suitable side runback. Double swing gates need clear arc space and careful allowance for slope and wind. The driveway should be measured before choosing either option.

Read the answer →

What if my neighbour will not pay half for a fence in Victoria?

Do not start shared work expecting automatic repayment. Get a clear quote, try to agree in writing and use the Victorian Fencing Notice process if needed. Our guide explains the steps.

Read the answer →

What are the current pool fencing rules in Victoria?

The pool must be registered, the correct standard depends on its construction date, and the barrier needs a periodic inspection and compliance certificate. Use our current state and council guide.

Read the answer →

Does a metal pool fence need electrical bonding?

Conductive metal close to a pool can require equipotential bonding under the Wiring Rules. A licensed electrician must assess and complete electrical bonding work.
