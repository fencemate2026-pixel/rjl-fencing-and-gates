Home/Pool fencing
Safety first

Compliance starts with the correct standard for your pool.

In Victoria, a pool or spa that can hold more than 30 centimetres of water needs a compliant safety barrier. The pool construction date helps decide which rules apply. Check the council record before changing the barrier.

Owners are responsible for registration, periodic inspection and lodging a current compliance certificate. Gates, latches, gaps and non-climbable zones must remain compliant after landscaping, furniture and planting are added.

Important: This is general information, not certification advice. Confirm the requirements for your property with your local council, registered building surveyor or pool safety inspector.

Glass pool fencing

Protect the pool without losing the view.

Frameless glass is suited to homeowners who want clear sightlines and minimal visual interruption around the pool. The layout still needs to resolve panel sizes, support, gate swing, latch position, ground levels, landscaping and non-climbable zones.

  • Frameless glass panels and self-closing gate coordination
  • Clear sightlines from the house and outdoor areas
  • Stainless steel hardware selected for the application
  • Site-specific interfaces with paving, decks and garden edges
  • Alternative aluminium or custom metal barriers where preferred

Pool & glass fencing FAQ

Questions before changing a pool barrier.

Does RJL install frameless glass pool fencing in Bundoora?

Yes. RJL can assess frameless glass and other pool-barrier systems around the pool layout, sightlines, gate position, surfaces and property-specific compliance requirements.

Can RJL replace an existing pool fence?

Yes. Existing gates, latches, panels and complete barriers can be assessed. The applicable standard and current site conditions should be confirmed before replacement work begins.

Does RJL install black aluminium pool fencing?

Yes. Powder-coated aluminium is one available pool-barrier option where its profile, spacing, gate hardware and installation suit the property requirements.

Who certifies a pool fence in Victoria?

Owners should use the relevant council process and an appropriately registered building surveyor or pool safety inspector for formal inspection and certification. RJL does not represent installation work as the compliance certificate itself.
