Glass and architectural aluminium

Pool Fencing & Safety Barriers

Keep clear views of the outdoor entertaining area while planning the pool boundary for the relevant Victorian safety requirements.

  • Frameless glass: Toughened safety glass panels and suitable stainless steel spigots, coordinated with the paving or deck structure.
  • Vertical batten screens: Powder-coated black aluminium batten barriers can provide an architectural finish around the pool.

Custom front entries

Automatic Double Swing Driveway Gates

Where a front boundary lacks sliding-gate runback, double swing gates may suit the site if there is enough arc space and ground clearance. We check the driveway geometry before choosing the movement.

  • Measured to the opening: Two custom gate leaves with coordinated posts, hinges, infill and ground clearances.
  • Automation options: Articulated-arm or other suitable motor systems are selected for the gate, pier geometry, weight and daily use.
  • Locks and stops: Suitable locks, latches, mechanical stops and gate hardware, including Locinox options where appropriate, help maintain reliable closure.

What helps when quoting a fence on changing ground levels?

Photograph the full boundary from both ends and show steps, retaining structures and access. Share any available level or site plans. The fence supports and any retained ground need to be assessed together.
