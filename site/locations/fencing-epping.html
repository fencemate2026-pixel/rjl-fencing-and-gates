Timber and Colorbond boundaries

Secure Side Gates & Privacy Fencing

A strong, lockable side entry helps separate the backyard from the street. We coordinate the pedestrian gate with the boundary fence, ground levels and access needs.

  • Steel-framed side gates: Custom pedestrian gates can use structural steel frames clad in vertical timber or Colorbond to match the fence.
  • Suitable locks and latches: Weather-resistant mechanical locks, latches or selected keypad systems are chosen for the household and gate.
  • Full privacy: Timber paling and genuine Colorbond boundaries are scoped around removal, post positions and neighbour access.

Smart access for wider openings

Automatic Sliding Driveway Gates

Make practical use of the front yard with a custom sliding driveway gate. The system is measured for the opening, side runback, support structure and vehicle approach.

  • Motor selection: Suitable operators can be sourced through Global Access, including selected Roger Automation and Key Automation systems, according to gate weight and use.
  • Access controls: Remote controls, keypads and compatible app-based entry can be integrated with the selected operator.
  • Obstruction safeguards: Safety beams, detection functions, travel limits and manual release are configured and tested for the installation.

Ground levels and structural planning

Integrated Boundary & Retaining Packages

Where neighbouring blocks have different levels, plan the retained soil and fence together. RJL can coordinate sleeper systems, drainage and the fence above as one package.

  • Timber or concrete sleepers: Material and height are selected to suit the retained soil, loads and property conditions.
  • Steel posts and footings: Post size, spacing and footing depth follow the site-specific structural requirements.
  • Drainage and approvals: Drainage, legal discharge, easements, access and required engineering or permits are checked before construction.
