Integrated structural boundaries

Concrete Sleeper Retaining Walls

Plan soil retention before building a fence across a sloping boundary. Concrete sleeper walls can create practical terraces and support a coordinated fence above.

  • Galvanised steel uprights: Structural steel posts and concrete footings are sized and positioned for the design loads and ground conditions.
  • Charcoal concrete sleepers: Concrete sleeper options provide a durable finish, with fence support coordinated as part of the wall design.
  • Drainage and engineering: Retained height, surcharge loads, drainage and property-specific approvals determine the final system.

Driveway geometry first

Sloped Driveway Sliding & Swing Gates

A steep approach needs more than a standard gate frame. RJL measures the incline, opening, clearances and support positions before fabrication.

  • Laser level measurements: The driveway pitch and ground levels guide the gate movement, frame and hardware selection.
  • Rising-hinge options: A suitable rising-hinge swing system may clear rising ground where geometry, loads and automation compatibility allow.
  • Cantilever options: Trackless sliding gates can avoid a ground track across the opening, provided there is sufficient runback and a suitable engineered support structure.

What should I show for a sloping driveway or boundary?

Include wide photos showing the slope, changes in level and vehicle approach. For a gate, show side space and the area behind the opening. These details help RJL assess travel, clearances and support requirements.
