Moonee Valley pool safety

Pool Fencing Compliance

Strathmore pool and spa owners arrange registration and barrier compliance inspections through Moonee Valley City Council. RJL installs glass and aluminium barriers to suit the applicable safety standard; an appropriately registered inspector assesses the completed barrier.

  • Glass and aluminium: Frameless glass panels and architectural black aluminium battens keep the pool visible while defining a safe boundary.
  • Gate hardware: Self-closing, self-latching gates open away from the pool, with latch positions and non-climbable zones assessed for the layout.
  • Formal certification: An appropriately registered inspector assesses the completed barrier; the owner lodges the certificate with council.

Custom gates and smart access

Driveway Gate Automation

Choose a driveway entry that fits the space available on your Strathmore property. Sliding gates need suitable side runback; swing gates need a clear opening arc and ground clearance. Matching pedestrian gates can complete the front boundary.
