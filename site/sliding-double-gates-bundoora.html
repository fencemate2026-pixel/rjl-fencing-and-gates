Driveway gate options

One opening. Several practical solutions.

Sliding driveway gates

A space-efficient choice where the gate can travel beside the front fence on a properly prepared track or engineered support system.

Double swing gates

Two coordinated leaves reduce the swing arc of each panel and suit homes with the clearance and levels for inward or approved outward movement.

Automatic gates

Motors, controls, safety beams, manual release and access devices selected around gate weight, travel and household use.

Manual driveway gates

A well-balanced manual gate with reliable latching can suit lower-use entries where automation is not required.

Pedestrian and side gates

Purpose-built entries with suitable locks, latches, closers and infill coordinated with the fence and driveway gate.

Complete front boundaries

Gate posts, front fencing, letterbox integration and access hardware can be planned as one consistent frontage.

Gate automation

Design the motor and safety system with the gate.

Automation performs best when the gate structure, travel, support and motor are properly matched. RJL can coordinate new automatic gates or assess whether an existing gate is suitable to motorise.

Driveway gate FAQ

Questions to resolve before fabrication.

Is a sliding or double swing gate better for my driveway?

A sliding gate needs suitable side runback and track or cantilever geometry. Double swing gates need clear arc space and careful allowance for driveway slope and wind. RJL checks the opening and use before recommending either system.

Can a new driveway gate be automated?

Yes. The gate, posts, hinges or track, motor size, power, safety devices and controls should be designed together for reliable movement.

Does RJL make pedestrian gates to match?

Yes. A matching pedestrian or side-access gate can be fabricated with the same infill, spacing, finish and hardware as the driveway gate and front fence.

Can RJL replace an existing driveway gate?

Yes. The site assessment checks the existing posts, footings, opening, ground levels, power and access before the replacement scope is confirmed.
