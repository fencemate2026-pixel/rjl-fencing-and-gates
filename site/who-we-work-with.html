Organisation enquiries

Common questions.

Does RJL quote fencing and gate works for local councils and schools?

Yes. RJL accepts enquiries from local councils, schools and education facilities for fencing, gates, controlled access and related works. The final scope depends on site requirements, approvals, access and scheduling.

Can RJL work with owners corporations and body corporates?

Yes. RJL can quote common-property fencing and gate projects for owners corporations and body corporates, including clear written scopes for stakeholder review.

Does RJL provide fencing for sporting clubs and community facilities?

Yes. Enquiries can cover perimeter fencing, gates, controlled access and associated works for sporting grounds, club facilities and community sites.

What information should an organisation include with an enquiry?

Provide the site address, contact person, required scope, approximate dimensions, photos, access restrictions, timing, drawings or specifications and any known approval or safety requirements.
