Multi-home development · Ringwood

Laser-cut fencing and an automated swing entry in Ringwood

Perimeter fencing and an automated swing gate were developed for a multi-home community, with custom laser-cut sheeting coordinated with its retaining walls.

Project delivered by RJL Commercial Group · Project photography

The brief

Designed around the site.

The project combined the boundary treatment and vehicle entry for a new multi-home development. The laser-cut pattern gives the steelwork a distinctive appearance while relating it to the retaining-wall design.

What the project includes.

  • Perimeter fencing for the multi-home community.
  • Custom laser-cut sheeting coordinated with the retaining walls.
  • Automated swing-gate entry using Key Automation equipment.
  • Electronic locking and GSM opening.
