A coordinated entry for a Richmond body corporate

A black slatted frontage brings the vehicle gate, pedestrian entry, intercom and mailbox bank together at this residential body corporate.

Project delivered by RJL Commercial Group · Project photography

The brief

Designed around the site.

A shared entrance has several jobs to do: admit vehicles, provide a separate pedestrian entry and keep everyday access hardware within the frontage. The entry coordinates those elements in one consistent black finish.

What the project includes.

  • Slatted sliding vehicle gate with matching fixed panels.
  • Separate pedestrian gate and apartment intercom.
  • Integrated mailbox bank.
  • Gate automation, safety devices and a warning light.

Plan the entry as a whole

A driveway gate is part of a frontage. Pedestrian movement, deliveries, mail and resident access need space in the same plan.
