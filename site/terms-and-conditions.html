Part 1 - Warranty Terms and Conditions

Company means RJL FENCING PTY. LTD.; Client means the customer named in the accepted quotation. These warranties apply to the specified work and products.

1. Warranty periods and cover

The Company named in the accepted quotation provides a 10-year warranty on its Australian-made custom steel fencing panels and gates, including their powdercoated finish, supplied and installed under that quotation. Cover starts at practical completion and applies to the original Client at the original installation address. The 10-year cover does not extend to timber, prefinished proprietary sheet fencing or other products unless expressly stated; their quoted warranties and statutory rights continue to apply.

ItemRJL warranty period

Cover includes defective steelwork, welds and structural fabrication, and powdercoating failure caused by defective materials, unsuitable original coating selection, preparation or application. Covered coating defects include peeling, blistering, flaking, loss of adhesion and premature or excessive fading or chalking attributable to a defect. Reasonable labour and materials needed to rectify a covered defect are included. A separate general workmanship period does not shorten this specific 10-year cover.

Motors, electronics, remotes and moving hardware have the separate warranty periods stated in the quotation. Their servicing requirements do not automatically cancel the steelwork or powdercoating warranty.

A longer motor or product warranty expressly promised in the accepted quotation will be honoured. Manufacturer warranties are additional where applicable and do not reduce the Company's own promises. The 2-year general workmanship period does not shorten the specific 10-year steelwork and coating cover.

Have the sliding gate motor and automation serviced at least every 12 months, and more frequently where the supplied manufacturer instructions or duty cycle require. Use the Company or a suitably qualified technician following the supplied service instructions. Keep the service record. Damage caused or materially worsened by missed or improper servicing is excluded to that extent; an unrelated defect or statutory right is not automatically cancelled.

2. Cleaning and inspection schedule

As a condition of this 10-year warranty, the Client must follow the Company's minimum care schedule below, or a more frequent product or site-specific schedule where supplied with the quotation. Manufacturer guidance can differ between coating products.

Site conditionsMinimum client care
Ordinary inland or suburban Victorian sitesWash all accessible coated surfaces at least every 6 months.
Coastal, industrial, poolside or heavily vegetated sites; areas sheltered from rainWash at least every 3 months and sooner whenever deposits accumulate.
All sitesInspect monthly and after storms, building work or chemical exposure. Remove sap, droppings, wet leaves and other contaminants promptly; do not wait for the next scheduled wash.

Coastal exposure depends on sea spray, wind and site conditions, rather than postcode alone. Rinse salt deposits promptly after significant salt spray. Heavy marine or chemical exposure may require a more frequent written site schedule. Rainfall alone is not an adequate cleaning program.

3. Approved washing method

Clean in shade when the coated surface is cool and below 25 degrees Celsius. Gently rinse away loose grit with clean fresh water. Wash with a soft cloth, sponge or soft-bristle brush and diluted, mild, non-abrasive, pH-neutral detergent. Work in small sections, without hard rubbing, and rinse thoroughly before detergent dries. Use a normal low-pressure garden hose. Keep water away from electrical enclosures and motor equipment, following their separate instructions.

4. Trees and plant deposits at Victorian sites

Tree-related risks depend on the material deposited, its concentration, contact time and the coating system. The examples below identify conditions requiring attention; they do not mean that every tree of a named species damages powdercoating. A nearby tree, by itself, does not invalidate the warranty.

Trees or exposureWhat the Client must watch for and remove
Gums and eucalyptsSap or gum deposits, bark, wet leaf litter and tannin-stained runoff accumulating on rails, panels or around post bases.
Pines and resin-producing conifersSticky resin deposits and needles or other debris lodged in rails, joints and drainage points.
Oaks and other trees dropping tannin-containing leavesWet leaves and brown runoff left on horizontal surfaces or trapped against the steelwork, especially during autumn and winter.
Fruiting or berry-bearing trees and vinesFallen fruit, berries and decomposing plant material staining surfaces or retaining moisture.
Trees or shrubs with sap-feeding insectsSticky honeydew deposits, associated dirt or sooty growth, and deposits beneath overhanging branches.
Any overhanging vegetationBranches rubbing the coating, climbers attached to the fence, and bird or animal droppings.

Inspect areas beneath trees more often during active sap or resin fall, leaf fall and after wind or rain. Remove loose plant material carefully. Soften fresh deposits using clean water and mild detergent, then gently lift them with a soft wet cloth. Do not scrape, dry-scrub or chip off hardened sap. If ordinary washing does not remove a deposit, ask the Company for instructions for the actual coating before using a solvent or stain remover.

Keep branches and climbers from rubbing or growing through the fence where lawfully and safely practicable. This clause does not require the removal or unlawful pruning of a protected tree. Increase cleaning where vegetation cannot be kept clear. Damage demonstrably caused by deposits left untreated, abrasion or trapped moisture is subject to clause 10.

5. Gardens irrigation and moisture

  • Keep above-ground coated components clear of soil, mulch, compost, grass clippings, manure, wet leaves and stored materials. Do not raise garden levels or bury bottom rails, panels or above-ground post surfaces. Maintain the clearance provided at installation; designed below-ground post embedment is not prohibited.
  • Keep drainage and any designed drain or vent openings clear. Do not allow prolonged ponding or alter drainage so that water remains against the steelwork. Direct sprinklers away from the fence and rinse off irrigation residues, fertilisers, pesticides and pool chemicals promptly.
  • Avoid sustained contact with animal urine, agricultural chemicals, bore-water deposits and pool or spa water. Prevent branches, equipment, bins and garden tools from repeatedly rubbing or striking the finish.

6. Cleaning products and treatments to avoid

Unless expressly approved for the actual finish in written instructions from the Company and coating manufacturer, the Client must not use:

  • Pressure washers, concentrated water jets, steam cleaners, sandblasting or other blasting methods.
  • Wire brushes, steel wool, abrasive pads, melamine cleaning pads, sandpaper, scraping blades, cutting compounds or abrasive polishes.
  • Brick or concrete acid, strong acidic or alkaline cleaners, bleach or chlorine cleaners, caustic soda, oven cleaner or aggressive degreasers.
  • Paint thinners, acetone, petrol, kerosene, turpentine, white spirits, citrus solvent cleaners, paint strippers or unapproved graffiti, tar and adhesive removers.
  • Unapproved waxes, sealants, ceramic coatings, repainting products, rust converters, chemical treatments or adhesive films.

Cleaning advice differs between coating systems. Do not assume that a product suitable for automotive paint, another fence or a different powder brand is suitable here. Use a solvent only under the written instructions for the supplied finish, test an inconspicuous area and rinse as directed. Prevent sunscreen, insect spray and other personal or household chemicals from remaining on coated surfaces; wash accidental deposits off promptly.

7. Building work and later alterations

  • Protect the fence during landscaping, concreting, rendering, painting, welding and grinding nearby. Prevent cement slurry, mortar, paint, metal filings and hot sparks from contacting the coating. Remove contamination before it dries, using the approved method; seek advice where removal is difficult.
  • Do not drill, cut, grind, weld, bend or otherwise modify coated steelwork, add incompatible fixings or dissimilar metal attachments, or repaint it without first obtaining repair and protection instructions from the Company.
  • Use only approved temporary protection. Remove tapes and films within their stated time limits. Do not trap moisture beneath wrapping or allow adhesive residue to bake onto the surface.

Any protection or repair needed because of the Company's own installation or modification remains the Company's responsibility. This section does not exclude an original defect merely because the work was cut, drilled or welded during installation.

8. Inspections damage and safe use

During the monthly inspection, check accessible welds, joints, post bases, fasteners and coated edges for chips, scratches, loose parts, rust spots, blistering or peeling. Photograph and report developing problems promptly. Do not remove sound coating to investigate rust, and do not paint over a suspected defect before it is assessed. Damage exposing steel needs prompt advice and an appropriate compatible repair to limit spreading corrosion.

Use fences and gates for their designed purpose. Do not climb, swing on or force gates, overload them, hang signs or other loads without approval, or use a fence as a retaining wall unless designed for that purpose. Keep gate tracks and moving clearances free of debris. Arrange mechanical or electrical work through a suitably qualified person and follow the separate gate maintenance instructions.

9. Maintenance records

Keep a simple dated record of washing, inspections, products used, reported damage and repairs. Photos, a calendar entry or a property maintenance log are suitable. The Client may carry out routine cleaning; paid servicing or a cleaning contractor's invoice is not required for the steelwork and coating warranty. Supply available records when a claim is assessed. Missing paperwork alone does not automatically invalidate the whole warranty.

10. Exclusions and assessment of cause

Under this voluntary warranty, the Company does not cover the affected damage to the extent that it is caused or materially worsened by failure to carry out the stated maintenance; accumulated sap, resin, tannins, droppings or other contamination; chemical exposure; abrasion, chips or impact; unauthorised alterations; misuse; or prolonged moisture contact caused by later landscaping, blocked drainage or changed site conditions.

The same principle applies to damage caused by vehicle impact, vandalism, forced entry, fire, flood, storm debris, animal damage, or ground movement, heave or settlement unrelated to a defect in the Company's design or work. Ordinary intended outdoor exposure is not, by itself, an exclusion. The Company must explain the factual basis of any excluded cause it relies on. An exclusion applies to the affected damage, not automatically to unrelated components or defects.

Normal wear of consumables, including batteries, lamps and worn moving parts, is excluded, but a manufacturing defect is not ordinary wear. Existing or third-party equipment not supplied by the Company is outside this voluntary warranty. For equipment the Company did supply, third-party manufacture alone does not exclude cover. Electrical surges, lightning, pest damage, water exposure or network outages are excluded only to the extent they cause the claimed fault and are not attributable to a defect in the Company's own goods or work.

11. Fading appearance and corrosion

Gradual weathering can include changes in colour or gloss and some chalking. The finish is not promised to remain identical to its new appearance throughout the 10 years. Whether a change is ordinary weathering or a covered defect must be assessed against the actual product, the site exposure, reasonable durability expectations and any relevant performance specification supplied with the quotation. Normal sunlight, coastal location or the mere presence of trees does not automatically cancel cover.

Premature fading, excessive chalking, peeling or corrosion caused by defective original coating selection, preparation, application or installation remains assessable under this warranty. Surface deposits or stains must not automatically be treated as a coating failure, and genuine coating failure must not automatically be dismissed as normal weathering. Screen images, lighting, separate batches and ageing can affect apparent colour; a reasonable assessment may require a clean surface and inspection in consistent conditions.

Notify the Company promptly after discovering a suspected defect, with the site address, invoice number or other reasonable proof of purchase, a description, photographs where possible and available maintenance records. Defects must arise within the 10-year voluntary warranty period. Statutory rights are not restricted to that period. Allow reasonable access for inspection. Contact the Company before elective remedial work so it can assess the cause; reasonable urgent work to make the site safe or prevent further damage does not automatically cancel cover. Preserve evidence where practicable.

The Company will investigate within a reasonable time and explain its assessment. For a covered defect, it will repair, recoat or replace the affected work as appropriate, without charging for necessary labour or materials, subject to any different remedy required by law. Repairs under this voluntary warranty remain covered for the balance of the original period, without limiting statutory rights. Manufacturer approval is not a substitute for the Company honouring its own warranty.

The Company bears reasonable expenses of making a successful warranty claim. Send receipts or other reasonable evidence of those expenses to the claims contact below for reimbursement. Advise anticipated transport, access or other unusual costs beforehand where practicable. Any charge for agreed work outside warranty must be disclosed and agreed before it is incurred. Statutory rights to recover reasonable expenses are preserved.

Send the claim to the Company named in your quotation using the contact details in this booklet. For a gate creating an immediate hazard, stop using it, keep people clear and contact the Company. Do not attempt electrical or mechanical repairs yourself.
