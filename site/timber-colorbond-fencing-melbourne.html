Boundary fencing specialists

Premium timber and Colorbond boundaries, planned from the ground up.

RJL Fencing and Gates installs premium timber and Colorbond boundary fencing across Melbourne’s northern suburbs. Operating from our Thomastown headquarters, our family-led team coordinates the replacement work, including laser ground-level measurements, tight site clearances and careful neighbour coordination. We build durable, private boundaries to suit the soil, levels and architectural style of each property.

  • Neighbour-friendly coordination: Clear quotes and communication between adjoining owners support the Victorian Fences Act process.
  • Colorbond boundary specialists: Low-maintenance steel privacy fencing in current profiles and colours, including Monument and Woodland Grey.
  • Premium treated timber: Horizontal timber panels, classic vertical paling and capped fences, with steel-post options to suit the project.
  • Integrated retaining solutions: Concrete or timber sleeper systems can be coordinated with your boundary fence where ground levels differ, subject to drainage, engineering and approvals.
Choose for the property

Two materials. Different strengths.

Timber fencing brings natural warmth and flexibility to traditional and contemporary homes. It can be stepped through uneven ground, shaped to the streetscape and finished in a broad range of paling, picket, slat and feature styles.

Colorbond fencing provides full privacy, a consistent factory finish and low day-to-day maintenance. The system works particularly well where a clean colour match with roofing, gutters or other exterior finishes is important.

  • New boundary fences, replacement fences and neighbour-shared projects
  • Treated-pine paling, capped, exposed-post, picket and slat options
  • Colorbond steel fencing in a broad range of current colours
  • Site demolition, disposal, access and ground levels included in scoping
  • Matching pedestrian gates and driveway-gate integration available

Before pricing

What makes a quote accurate.

Fence pricing depends on much more than linear metres. Access, demolition, retaining conditions, underground services, rock, vegetation, levels, corners, gate openings and neighbour coordination all affect the real scope.

  • Property address and best contact details
  • Approximate fence length and preferred height
  • Photos looking along both sides of the boundary
  • Any gates, retaining walls, trees, sheds or access limitations
  • Your preferred material, colour or a reference image

If you and your neighbour have not agreed on the cost, read what to do when a neighbour will not pay half before work starts.

Timber & Colorbond FAQ

Questions before replacing a boundary fence.

Does RJL replace old timber fences?

Yes. The quote can include removal and disposal of the existing fence, new posts and rails, paling or feature finishes, gates and practical access requirements.

Is timber or Colorbond better for a boundary fence?

Timber offers flexible detailing and a natural appearance. Colorbond provides full privacy, consistent colour and low day-to-day maintenance. Access, levels, neighbouring structures and the preferred appearance influence the choice.

Can a Colorbond fence be installed above a retaining wall?

It may be possible, but the retaining system, post support, wind load, drainage and total boundary height must be planned together for the actual site.

What should I send for a fencing quote?

Send the suburb, approximate length and height, photos along both sides of the boundary, access details, demolition needs, gates, retaining conditions and your preferred material or colour.
